    Cliff Notes

    8 questions to answer
    before you upgrade.

    Bring these to your next leadership meeting. Any answer that comes back "we don't know" is the place to start.

    1. How many customizations do we run, and which ones does the business still use?
    2. Which integrations touch IFS, and who owns each one?
    3. Who built our key customizations, and are they still here?
    4. What does extended support cost us each year until March 2028?
    5. When is our slow season, and how many of those windows are left before the cliff?
    6. Are our users on Enterprise Explorer or Aurena today?
    7. Which regulatory or customer requirements (traceability, tax, payroll) need changes in the next two years?
    8. Do we want IFS to host IFS Cloud, or will we run it ourselves?
